## Overview

Lab 2 applies Agile/Scrum project management principles using **Atlassian Jira** to plan, estimate, prioritize, and execute sprints for the **Lab Equipment & Slot Reservation Portal**.

### Epics Defined:
1. **User Management & Authentication**: User Registration (`LEASR-1`), User Login (`LEASR-2`).
2. **Lab & Equipment Management**: Manage Laboratories (`LEASR-3`), Manage Equipment (`LEASR-4`), View Equipment Details (`LEASR-6`), Check Equipment Availability (`LEASR-7`), Request Equipment (`LEASR-8`).
3. **Slot Reservation Management**: View Available Slots (`LEASR-5`), Manage Reservations (`LEASR-10`), Reserve a Lab Slot (`LEASR-12`), Cancel / Reschedule Slot (`LEASR-5`).
4. **Request & Approval Workflow**: Technician Approval (`LEASR-9`), Reservation Status Tracking (`LEASR-10`), Request Equipment (`LEASR-16`).
5. **Notifications & Reminders**: Email / In-App Notifications (`LEASR-11`), Slot Reminder Alerts (`LEASR-12`).
6. **Reports & Analytics**: Usage Reports (`LEASR-13`), Equipment Utilization Report (`LEASR-14`), Export Reports (`LEASR-15`).
